# ProfitLands — integrated prelaunch development candidate

Status: **Multiplayer V2 deployed in production on November 6, 2026 and running with writes enabled. The singleplayer experience remains available, but full solo/online parity and CrazyGames approval are NOT yet verified.**
Updated: November 6, 2026

## New verified feature work (development branch; deploy only after CI)
- **Economy:** 18 simulated regional cities with verified location rent/demand and priced relocation; eight securities (four new IPOs) with news-driven volatility, unlock requirements and 15-minute quotes; market-sensitive returns in the six higher corporate tiers. Existing location-less companies and the local corporation retain their baseline behavior.
- **Progression:** server-owned repeatable empire ascension, with a rising worth requirement, income bonus, preserved lifetime trophies and an explicit asset-reset confirmation. This is a repeatable model, **not unlimited currency or database storage**.
- **Contracts and achievements:** eight verified city work orders (four multi-step) with action-order checking; authenticated highest-rank online trophy merge across three regions without trusting local scores.
- **Testing:** live Firebase/Firestore two-session save recovery, owner-only isolation and stale-write rejection passed; temporary users and test documents deleted. Local V2 concurrency test passed with 32 accounts, 64 overlapping purchases and exact replay receipts. Packaged CrazyGames v3 local Chrome QA passed gameplay/purchase and 907x510 and 320x568 viewport checks. **No CrazyGames Developer Portal approval and no hosted 64-player quota guarantee.**

## What is implemented in this candidate

- Three independent regional Cloudflare/D1 multiplayer worlds with server-validated sign-in, ledgers, purchases, 8 industry types, rankings, optional PvP, and player aliases.
- Automated regional chat: server-side masking of common profanity and slurs into `***`, disguised spelling handling, temporary abuse mutes, spam limits, contact-detail blocking, report/block controls, optional privileged human review, and an emergency shutdown switch. Filter misses and false positives remain possible.
- Firebase private **singleplayer** cloud saves: revision-checked Firestore transactions, device/cloud conflict prompts, local recovery copies, account-owner isolation, and an explicit fresh-account start when a prior account's local progress exists. Online multiplayer economies are persisted in Cloudflare D1, not in the Firebase solo `last-game` slot.
- Expanded server-owned multiplayer economy with company staff, managers, marketing, efficiency, company branches, **branch upgrades that increase genuine passive income**, inventory purchases and actual time-based sales, price adjustments, auto-restocking, warehouses, trucks, stock transfers and **multiple concurrent supply routes (one route per paid truck)**. Legacy single-route records migrate without losing scheduled deliveries; selling a company removes or reindexes affected routes.
- Online portfolio: eight simulated securities (the original four plus four unlockable public offerings), deterministic news- and sector-sensitive fifteen-minute quotes, volatility and price-change display, market-price-valued holdings and rankings, server-authorized trades, loans/repayments, property/rental holdings, and technology research. Mark-to-market movements are allocated to UTC daily/weekly periods; refreshes cannot double-count them.
- **Seven corporate tiers from singleplayer:** local through Global Empire, with identical base purchase prices, unlock thresholds, and positive baseline operating margins; the regional server enforces cash, wealth and ownership checks and includes assets and passive income in world rankings. Higher corporate tiers now use modest server-derived daily demand volatility and regional events, while the local tier preserves legacy payout behavior.
- **All 35 singleplayer countries:** generated into worker and Firebase economy modules from the canonical country model. The online country selector includes every country; server-side baseline business margins respond to the original per-country demand, revenue, wage, rent and sector strength factors. Country-switching is locked after expansion/financial activity.
- **Daily regional economy events:** a deterministic 24-day regional news cycle including all 18 singleplayer event themes plus additional scenarios, with balanced sector-specific effects on server-settled earnings. Existing current-week event timing is preserved. The Management tab displays the scenario and refreshes cannot collect income twice. The simplified real-UTC daily cadence still differs from solo's accelerated game days and multi-day event durations.
- **City work orders:** eight server-owned templates: four original one-step orders and four two-stage orders covering hiring, supply, logistics and investment. Only the correct, newly completed paid action advances each required stage in order; claims are replay-safe and capped at one reward per UTC day.
- **Debt recovery:** new online loans carry a 30-day real-world deadline; opt-in repayment automatically uses 15% of earned income, with 40% mandatory repayments from subsequent earnings after the due date. Existing legacy loans are grandfathered and do not acquire an unannounced deadline. Debt repayments conserve net worth and cannot be claimed as lost ranking performance.
- **Guarded online bankruptcy:** only insolvent players with overdue new loans may liquidate their virtual businesses, assets and liabilities. Creditors consume the liquidation pool first, and the owner receives a fixed $500 game restart allowance. Multiplayer trades and replayed requests remain atomic and idempotent; 30-day recovery and 90-day new-credit cooldowns prevent repetitive loan forgiveness. Lifetime trophy history and paid transaction receipts are preserved. Cross-player marketplace purchases are blocked while the buyer has outstanding loan debt, preventing loan proceeds being transferred to another player before insolvency. This is a bounded game recovery mechanic, not complete singleplayer financial distress simulation.
- **Corporate-margin parity:** the Conglomerate tier now uses the original singleplayer $320m revenue / $250m expense baseline, rather than one-tenth of that margin.
- **Corporate-income-tax ledger:** server-verified annual income-tax assessments at the selected country's rate, based on tracked operating earnings less inventory procurement expenses. Stock purchases/sales and borrowed principal do not count as operating profit. A bill becomes an outstanding liability until the player explicitly pays it with server-authorized cash. Online fiscal years are 365 **real UTC days**, unlike accelerated singleplayer game days; full singleplayer financial simulation is still missing.
- Authenticated, same-region company marketplace: players choose to list companies at bounded prices, others can acquire them with atomic cash/asset transfers and transaction replay protection.
- The online server derives earned trophy ranks across the same 8 industry categories × 16 tracks × 1,024 possible ranks (131,072 combinations); these ranks are **not yet synchronized** into the singleplayer Firebase account-wide trophy collection.
- The World interface has Management, Supply & Logistics, Stock Market, Finance & Property, and Research & Contracts controls. All online mutations pass through the verified backend; local singleplayer money is not authoritative for World operations.
- Isolated CrazyGames HTML5 build with platform SDK adapter and basic automated packaging tests; no existing public web account configuration is removed from the regular release.

## Remaining acceptance gaps (multiplayer V2 production is live)

1. **Full singleplayer parity remains incomplete.** The draft multiplayer backend now permits 80 companies, 16 sites/company and 64 players/region, and compresses larger D1 ledgers without overwriting original saves. These are still bounded below singleplayer's near-unlimited growth. Singleplayer allows much larger locations and advanced features. Missing or incomplete systems include singleplayer's richer business-failure thresholds and accelerated fiscal simulation (a safe overdue-loan bankruptcy recovery now exists), the full solo news-event durations and multi-day simulation (all 18 solo event themes are now available in a rotating online schedule), the full solo city catalog beyond 18 modeled online cities, detailed advancements and location-level labor/supply economics, a trusted offline-to-online trophy ownership merge, and the accelerated singleplayer time and financial formulas. Eight modeled stocks including four IPOs, higher-tier corporation volatility, eight staged work orders, regional trophy summaries and repeatable ascension now exist in the development branch. This branch adds real features, **not full parity**.
2. **Scalability and migration:** V2 normalized D1 tables are now implemented behind an opt-in flag: separate per-player data, per-action receipts and small region metadata with transaction-checked revisions. Existing world rows remain untouched as rollback snapshots; an operator-only two-factor temporary migration Worker and read-only maintenance mode support safe cutover. Ordinary operations load only the relevant account(s) and receipt, and an atomic D1 batch updates both ends of cross-player trades. **V2 is deployed and the existing live D1 has been migrated**, preserving all original players and receipts. A legitimate two-account Firebase test exercised the public Worker, purchased and transferred a company with replay protection, and cleaned up both test accounts from Auth and D1. A 32-account/64-overlapping-purchase local V2 SQLite stress test passed; production Cloudflare concurrency and free-tier quotas remain unverified. Ordinary unrelated player purchases use independent per-player CAS and do not update the region metadata revision; regional chat and admission still serialize on a small metadata revision. Very high concurrent player counts remain unverified on actual Cloudflare D1 and will require load testing and potentially further sharding. See `cloudflare/V2_STORAGE_MIGRATION.md`.
3. **Cloud saves and authentic accounts:** two genuine Firebase accounts and two independent sessions verified live Firestore saved-game permissions, recovery and stale-write rejection; browser-level cross-device UX remains to test. Two genuine temporary email/password Firebase accounts passed both a local Worker/D1 transaction test and a live V2 D1 marketplace transaction test. Both accounts and all their live D1 records were deleted after QA. Google/Firebase authentication settings were not weakened. Actual two-device browser UX and offline reconnection conflicts still need testing. Firestore backend's two-session recovery and stale-write rejection have been verified. Preserve backups and recovery paths.
4. **Chat:** automatic filtering does not catch every disguise or contextual harassment. Reports, review and incident appeals remain necessary; live chat is active but automated filtering is not a replacement for abuse response or multi-account chat testing. Use `CHAT_DISABLED=true` to shut it off immediately.
5. **Regional multiplayer:** Live Worker reports version 6, 64 accounts/region and writable status. The V2 normalized migration was rehearsed against a private, verified real-world SQL export (1 legacy region, 1 account, 4 receipts) and would create two missing regions in memory. **The production V2 cutover is complete.** A frozen production backup was checked, migrated into normalized tables, and compared against the original D1 records (zero player or receipt mismatches); the original legacy ledger is retained for audit. Live two-account sign-in and marketplace purchase/transfer/replay tests succeeded. Live multi-user chat/PvP and high-load free-tier testing are still not complete.
6. **CrazyGames:** the isolated 99-file upload has one v3 SDK script and passes local headless Chrome gameplay, purchase and iframe/mobile layout checks. CrazyGames Developer Portal preview, hosted APS restore and platform approval remain unverified; passing a local build is not approval.
7. **UI accessibility/performance:** real-world mobile, keyboard, 320px layout, reload and save recovery tests are needed for the expanded online UI.

## Release principle

Preserve the private D1 backups and original legacy world rows; do not activate billing, wipe data, promise unlimited online scaling or announce full singleplayer parity. The multiplayer production migration and authenticated cross-account transaction acceptance are complete, but CrazyGames submission, broad high-load performance and several solo/online gameplay systems still require work.
